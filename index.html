Why Ghana, why now

Ghana is West Africa's most accessible entry point — stable, English-speaking, and welcoming, with a growing "Year of Return" pilgrimage drawing the diaspora home.

In one trip: 500-year-old coastal fortresses, rainforest canopy walks, savanna elephants, kente-weaving villages, and some of the region's best-kept beaches. This lookbook moves coast to savanna — Cape Coast, Accra, Kumasi, Mole — as a route you can actually drive.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit

November–March: dry season, cooler nights, easiest travel between regions.

Getting around

Domestic flights Accra–Kumasi–Tamale save time; road trips reveal the most.

Entry

Most visitors need a visa or e-visa in advance — apply at least a few weeks ahead.

Suggested route

Accra → Cape Coast → Kakum → Kumasi → Mole, 10–14 days end to end.
